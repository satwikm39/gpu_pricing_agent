# Executive Summary

## The Educational Narrative
Welcome to the GPU Pricing Agent simulation! In this module, we are placing students in the seat of a Cloud Executive managing a fleet of highly sought-after GPUs (H100s, A100s, L40S, etc.). 

The core lesson we are trying to show students is the delicate balance between **deterministic mathematics**, **dynamic market economics**, and **AI Governance**.

### What Students Will Experience
1. **The Limits of Static Math**: A standard calculator can give you a base price based on cost. But what happens when the global inventory of H100s drops below 5%? Static math leaves money on the table, while unbridled dynamic pricing can ruin customer trust.
2. **The Power of Agentic AI**: Students will employ an autonomous LLM-powered agent to enforce business policies (like surge pricing during scarcity, or evicting cheap Spot instances to make room for full-paying On-Demand customers).
3. **The Necessity of "Glass-Box" Explainability**: When an AI suddenly triples the price of a GPU or cancels a workload, customers will get angry. The system demonstrates *Glass-Box AI*—the agent doesn't just return a price; it returns a natural language, semantic explanation justifying its decision to the end-user, maintaining transparency from the service provider.
4. **Balancing Revenue vs. Trust**: Students will tweak policy thresholds (e.g., Margin Floors, Eviction Deltas). If they are too aggressive, they might maximize short-term ROI but lose customer trust scores. If they are too conservative, they won't recover hardware depreciation costs.

By interacting with the simulation, students witness firsthand how abstract financial policies translate into real-time autonomous decisions, emphasizing the crucial importance of guardrails when deploying Agentic AI in enterprise scenarios.
